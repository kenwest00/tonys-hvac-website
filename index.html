More Than a Quick Look-Over

A Real Maintenance Check.
Not a Sales Pitch.

Your system may look fine right up until the day it cannot keep up. Synergy checks the essentials, explains the findings clearly, and helps you stay ahead of avoidable breakdowns.

✦

Clean the Coils

Cleaner coils can help your system transfer heat more efficiently, reduce unnecessary strain, and support stronger cooling performance.

◈

Check Refrigerant Performance

We check refrigerant operation and key system readings to help identify issues that may contribute to weak airflow, warm air, or longer run times.

◉

Check the Capacitor

This component helps critical HVAC parts start and run correctly. Catching a weak capacitor early may help prevent a sudden no-cool or no-heat call.

↻

Check Blower-Motor Amperage

Measuring the blower motor’s electrical draw can help identify whether it is working harder than it should, which may affect airflow and reliability.

↔

Inspect Airflow & Visible Wear

A practical look at airflow and visible system condition can reveal comfort issues before they become harder—and more expensive—to ignore.
